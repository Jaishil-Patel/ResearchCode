Room Plotter

Enter the room shell, openings and furniture from your tape measurements, sanity-check the layout in 3D, then copy the generated _spec.yaml straight into the repo.

● Showing example measurements. Replace them with your own — everything below saves to this browser automatically as you type.

1Room shell

Stand in one corner first — that's world (0,0), the x‑axis runs along the long wall, y along the short wall. Every other measurement is taken from there.

2Openings

One row per door or low window. start/end are distances along that wall from your origin corner; bottom/top are heights off the floor (leave a door's top at the room height).

3Furniture

Bounding box only — bring the exact shape in via the photogrammetry scan, not here. Centre is the box's middle, from the origin corner; yaw only matters if it sits at an angle.

Checks

Catches the kind of mistake that only shows up later — a door taller than the room, a desk that pokes through a wall.
